Consultoria Técnica Internacional e Gestão de Projetos

BORAX is a premier independent consulting firm specializing in engineering infrastructure, geological subcontracting, restoration project advisory, and European exchange frameworks.

BORAX; mühendislik altyapıları, jeolojik saha analizleri, restorasyon projelerinde alt yüklenicilik ve Avrupa değişim programlarında uzmanlaşmış bağımsız bir danışmanlık firmasıdır.

BORAX é uma empresa líder de consultoria independente especializada em infraestrutura de engenharia, subcontratação geológica e projetos de intercâmbio europeu.

Geological Consulting Subcontractor

Site & Technical Subcontracting

Providing high-level geological consulting, subcontracting site support, and comprehensive GIS reporting for main contractors in major construction and restoration developments.

İnşaat, altyapı ve restorasyon projelerinde ana yüklenici firmalara profesyonel jeolojik danışmanlık, zemin etüdü ve teknik alt yüklenici çözümleri sunuyoruz.

Prestação de consultoria geológica profissional e suporte de subcontratação em projetos de construção e restauração.

  • Geotechnical Surveying & Reporting: Comprehensive soil mechanics and geological assessments.
  • GIS & Spatial Analysis: Advanced Geographic Information System mapping and hazard analysis.
  • Restoration Subcontracting Support: Specialized site execution for historical structures and site restoration.
  • Site Hazard Assessments: Environmental and geological risk evaluations for main contractors.

European Exchange Projects Consulting

Grant & Mobility Consultancy

Strategic end-to-end consulting, grant management, and operational coordination for European youth mobility, volunteering, and international exchange programs.

Avrupa gençlik, eğitim ve kültürel değişim projelerinde kurumlara ve bireylere stratejik danışmanlık, proje ortaklığı ve operasyonel rehberlik sağlıyoruz.

Consultoria estratégica e gestão para programas de mobilidade de jovens e projetos de intercâmbio europeu.

  • European Solidarity Corps (ESC): Program accreditation, application strategy, and project oversight.
  • International WorkCamps: Placement coordination and international volunteer project management.
  • SummerCamps & Youth Exchanges: Organizational support for educational summer programs.
